# Design discussion sources

Raw material from the team's design discussion on 16 October 2026, kept as
received. Juro Oravec (software engineer) and Soňa (a doctor with
first-hand MDT experience; the product is based on her experience).

- `2026-10-16-chatgpt-brainstorm-sona.md`: Soňa's brainstorm with ChatGPT
  (Slovak), exported from ChatGPT. The oncology cases pasted into it are
  a synthetic sample (Juro, 16 October 2026).
- `2026-10-16-chatgpt-brainstorm-sona-pt2.jpeg`: a screenshot of one more
  part of that conversation, missing from the export: Soňa's visual
  direction (GLIDE-inspired clinical workspace).
- `Rakathon 1/2/3.elevenlabs.md`: transcripts of the team's spoken
  discussion (Slovak), made with `scripts/transcribe.py --language slk`.
  The `.json` next to each is the raw response. The audio (`.m4a`) is not
  in git. Speaker labels are automatic; which label is Juro and which is
  Soňa is in [`docs/design/PRODUCT.md`](../../design/PRODUCT.md).
- `Rakathon 4 - first UI feedback.elevenlabs.md`: transcript of Soňa's
  feedback on the first UI mock (about 7 minutes, same method and
  layout as recordings 1 to 3).
- `ui-screenshots/`: 11 screenshots of screen designs Soňa made with
  ChatGPT and shared during the feedback round (patient timeline, action
  items, MDT session, information flow, oncology summary toggle, patient
  database fields, attendance, anamnesis table, coordinator
  notifications). All patients and doctor names in them are fictional.
- `2026-10-16-juro-notes.md`: Juro's typed notes, verbatim.
- `2026-10-16-juro-paper-notes.md`: transcription of Juro's paper notes,
  from `open_questions.jpg`, `more_notes.jpg`, and `ui_scribbles.jpg`.

What was extracted from these, and where it went, is in
[`docs/design/PRODUCT.md`](../../design/PRODUCT.md).
